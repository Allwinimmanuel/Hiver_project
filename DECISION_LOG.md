# Technical Decision Log

This document records the major architectural, ML, and product decisions made during the development of the AmazonHelp AI Support Agent, fulfilling Phase 13 of the project requirements.

| ID | Decision | Reason | Trade-off / Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| DEC-01 | **Select @AmazonHelp brand.** | Amazon has a massive, highly varied presence in the TWCS dataset, ensuring we have enough examples for complex issues like Prime, Delivery, and Refunds. | Focuses solely on retail/logistics; might not generalize perfectly to SaaS or airline customer service intents. |
| DEC-02 | **Define 8 specific intents.** | Covers the vast majority of Amazon's support spectrum while leaving room for an explicit `other_unknown` and an explicit `customer_service_escalation`. | Very granular intents (e.g., separating "late delivery" from "lost delivery") are merged, losing some precision. |
| DEC-03 | **Use Weak Supervision Labeling.** | Manual labeling of 20,000+ tweets is impossible within the timeline. Weak supervision (keyword regex) allows rapid bootstrapping of training data. | Introduces label noise; the training set is not perfectly clean. |
| DEC-04 | **Select TF-IDF Vectorizer.** | Extremely fast, transparent, computationally cheap, and highly effective for short text with distinct keywords (e.g., "stolen"). | Cannot capture semantic meaning or out-of-vocabulary synonyms (e.g., "yeeted my parcel"). |
| DEC-05 | **Select Logistic Regression.** | Provides rapid, interpretable probabilities essential for confidence-based routing, and handles high-dimensional sparse data well. | Linear decision boundary; struggles with complex, multi-intent tweets compared to deep learning models. |
| DEC-06 | **Set 60% confidence threshold.** | A conservative threshold ensures ambiguous predictions are caught. If the model isn't reasonably sure, a human should look at it. | Increases the escalation rate (False Positives for escalation), requiring more human agent time. |
| DEC-07 | **Add Heuristic Escalation Overrides.** | ML models struggle to detect pure intent from highly sensitive keywords (e.g., "stolen", "human"). Hardcoded overrides guarantee 100% compliance on high-risk keywords. | Rigid. If a customer says "I am a human", they might be escalated unnecessarily. |
| DEC-08 | **Use hardcoded historical evidence.** | Constraining the LLM with exact, real historical resolutions prevents it from hallucinating non-existent policies or impossible delivery guarantees. | The agent cannot generate highly personalized dynamic solutions outside the hardcoded evidence. |
| DEC-09 | **Implement Fallback Reply Templates.** | OpenAI API keys might be invalid, rate-limited, or unavailable. Deterministic fallback templates ensure the UI never crashes or shows raw 500 errors. | Fallback replies are robotic and lack empathy compared to generative text. |
| DEC-10 | **Create a segregated Golden Set.** | A pristine 250-example set was manually verified and strictly removed from training. This ensures our evaluation metrics are not inflated by data leakage. | Takes time to manually verify; limits the amount of data available for training. |
| DEC-11 | **Analyze Class Imbalance.** | Essential to prove that a 76% accuracy is misleading. Over 70% of tweets are `other_unknown`, inflating the overall accuracy while masking poor recall on rare classes. | Requires complex metrics (Macro F1) that are harder for non-technical stakeholders to understand. |
| DEC-12 | **Use a 25-example human agreement sample.** | Evaluating the generative replies manually is extremely slow. 25 examples are sufficient to prove the human-in-the-loop evaluation framework exists and works. | 25 examples are not statistically significant for a robust production LLM-Judge calibration. |
| DEC-13 | **Avoid Full-Dataset Training.** | Training on 300,000+ rows takes too long for an intern assignment and isn't necessary to prove the architecture and evaluation pipeline. | The model is less robust than it could be. |
| DEC-14 | **Exclude Multi-Turn Memory.** | TWCS data is often just a single blast. Focusing on single-turn classification is the core of the routing problem. | Cannot handle customers who reply "No, that didn't help." |
| DEC-15 | **Select FastAPI and Streamlit.** | Decouples the backend logic from the frontend UI, simulating a real microservice architecture while providing a beautiful interactive demo. | More complex to run and manage than a single monolithic script. |
