# Golden Set Review Plan

## 1. Why the Original Automated Labeling was Insufficient
To bypass the manual labeling phase initially, we used a deterministic, regex-based keyword matching script (`09_auto_label.py`). However, human language on Twitter is extremely variable and noisy. 

Our subsequent quality audit revealed that our simple regex engine missed approximately 47% of actionable support requests simply because the customers phrased their issues slightly differently than expected (e.g., using "hasn't shipped yet" instead of "not shipped", or typing in another language). This resulted in an artificially inflated `other_unknown` count.

A benchmark dataset generated by rigid, flawed rules is useless for evaluating a machine learning model, because the model might actually correctly identify the intent, but be penalized for disagreeing with a flawed "Ground Truth".

## 2. Examples Requiring Human Review
During our audit sample of 30 items, we successfully flagged exactly **14 specific misclassified examples**. 

These 14 examples have been extracted and isolated into a dedicated review queue located at:
`data/gold/golden_set_review_queue.csv`

## 3. Ensuring Human-Verified Labels
To resolve this and guarantee the integrity of our dataset:
1. We have provided a structured CSV queue where a human reviewer must explicitly read the customer text, review the "suggested intent" provided by the audit, and write their final decision in the `human_decision` and `final_intent` columns.
2. We strictly avoided using an LLM to automatically generate or force these final decisions. 
3. The original Golden Set will **not** be modified until the human reviewer completes this queue and a script intentionally merges the verified corrections back into the main dataset.

## 4. Improving Evaluation Reliability
A "Golden Set" is meant to represent absolute, indisputable ground truth. By injecting human verification into the most ambiguous and problematic labels, we eliminate the bias introduced by our rule-based heuristics. When we finally build the AI agent in Milestone 4, its evaluation metrics (Accuracy, Precision, Recall) will be measured against a genuinely trustworthy benchmark.
